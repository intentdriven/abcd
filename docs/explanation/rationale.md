# Who abcd is for

AI coding is getting more powerful, yet one important group of people who most need that power — the domain experts, the *product thinkers*, the people who know *what should be built* — are currently underserved to be able to use AI coding directly. A **product thinker** is someone who holds the *why* of a product — who it is for and what it should do — without needing to command the *how* of the code. `abcd`'s bet is that a small team of three roles can close that gap: a product thinker who holds the *why*, a **facilitator** who translates the why into work that AI coding agents can act on, and the **team of AI agents** that builds it. The *product thinker* framing is credited to a [post by signulll](https://x.com/signulll/status/2030404483897815089).
