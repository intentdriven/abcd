The lab discipline (itd-2609251624540864) defines a lab as a throwaway world pinned at one commit: a detached clone with no remote, discarded at the end, with knowledge entering the record only through ceremony. A downstream brief-authoring lab ran in the real project repository, whose output (the brief) is the product being kept, and the procedure has no form for that case: its snapshot, record and discard stages all assume the world is thrown away.
