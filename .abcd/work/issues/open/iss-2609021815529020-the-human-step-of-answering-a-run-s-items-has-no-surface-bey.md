The human step of answering a run's items has no surface beyond the disposition command: a worksheet that walks the researcher through every outstanding item one reading at a time, offers the four states as the design allows them per position with the refusals built in (grounds required on every state but held, an exit condition on held, no default and nothing meaning already covered), loads the outstanding items from a markdown export of the reading records and returns one uniquely named answer file that a session records through the disposition command, was prototyped outside the record for the opening run. Whether it becomes a planned intent, and whether the site renders the outstanding items of every open run as a read-only page, is a maintainer decision after the first run shows what the form needs.
