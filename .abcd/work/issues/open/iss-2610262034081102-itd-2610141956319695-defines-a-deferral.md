itd-2610141956319695 defines a deferral as capturing a finding as its own record; the capture surface's deferral is a waiver written onto an existing open record past one cut

Under itd-2610141956319695 a deferral is always a capture: a deferred finding becomes an open issue of its own. The capture surface's deferring writes the release cut's waiver onto an open record that already exists, for a major or critical grade only, and carries it past one cut. A receipt reading deferred and a record carrying a deferral therefore name different acts under one word.
