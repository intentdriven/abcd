abcd's questions to the product thinker often lack a concrete example of what each option would mean in practice, and the trade-off between the options is not clear enough to choose from. In the interview of 15 November several questions had to be explained again before they could be answered. The asking rules (GRILL, the question check in abcd guard hook, and the question-drafter agent) limit a question's size and shape but ask for neither an example nor a stated trade-off. Not for the current work; to be worked on later.
