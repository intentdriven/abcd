Session naming (itd-2610111707085098) has no switch to turn it off: every session abcd's hooks run in is given a persona name unless the person names it by hand, and a person who does not want abcd to title their sessions has no way to say so short of naming every session themselves. The planning of 2026-10-15 left the switch out of scope to keep the build small.
