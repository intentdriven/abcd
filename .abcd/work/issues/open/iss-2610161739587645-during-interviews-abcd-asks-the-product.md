During interviews abcd asks the product thinker and the technical facilitator questions that have one clearly best answer. On 2026-10-16 the product thinker said to ask only where there is a clear trade-off, or several equally valid options. The GRILL rule already says a decision with one defensible answer is recorded, not asked. But the planning interview's own steps in commands/intent.md tell the host to confirm the press release, ask for the mechanism claim, the scope conditions and the grounds, and walk every acceptance criterion one question at a time, so a host following the page asks single-answer questions by design. The question-drafter agent drafts whatever it is handed and does not push back when only one answer is defensible.
