The interview rule makes the agent offer options that are not real choices, and asks a question where only one answer is sound. The GRILL domain in .abcd/rules.json says options widen and never recommend, no starred default, the null answer always available, and every question through the interactive tool one at a time. Followed literally on 2026-09-20 during the peer-listing interview, that produced a question whose three options were one sound answer beside two the record's own principle (loud-staging) already rules out, and the product thinker answered "why these options? Isn't 1 the obvious choice here?". The rule as written confuses two things: not steering the human (right) and manufacturing alternatives so a question has three of them (wrong). Ruled by the product thinker on 2026-09-20: the rule should not offer nonsense options; if there are no sensible alternatives, do not show them; if there is a single genuine answer, record it with the reason and do not put the question to the product thinker or the technical facilitator at all. Wanted: the GRILL domain reworded to say that a question is asked only where two or more answers are each defensible on the record, that the option set is exactly those answers plus the null answer, and that a decision with one defensible answer is recorded as a decision line naming why no question was asked; the itd-201 draft (every question asked one at a time, in plain language) is where the surface rule lives and should carry the same change.
