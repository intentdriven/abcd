Whether a days-long coordinator session is cheaper and as reliable under a lower auto-compact limit or under handover to a fresh session is unmeasured. In the seven days to 4 Nov 2026 main sessions were 45 percent of usage on API prices, six ran over 72 hours, and the long ones averaged 200k to 232k tokens of context per call; context size, not hours open, is what costs. A review of this expects about a tenth of the week, not half, and rotation carries its own cost: every fresh session starts cold and a handover can lose items.
