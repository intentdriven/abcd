Two accounts cannot add a verb at the same time, since the hand-kept lists a new verb must join are reserved whole by any long lane that touches one line of them: on 2026-11-25 a reservation for a new verb was refused (exit 3) on internal/core/surface/examples.go, and the live reservations of three other lanes held internal/surface/cli/cli.go, internal/core/refusal/refusal.go, its raiser test and code list, two drain pages, and the configuration chapter, until the next morning. A file marked generated is shared with a warning; these are written by hand, one line per verb, and are judged as any file is.
