itd-2609020625405251 makes a condition's standing the latest block naming it; the intent chapter lets a reading-occasioned block stand over a later verdict that does not name its occasion.

A premise contradiction (severity minor) found by the cross-document consistency pass.

- End B: `.abcd/development/brief/04-surfaces/05-intent.md:331` — "A condition's standing is its latest reading-occasioned block where it has one, and otherwise its latest verdict block"

The condition intent says a condition's standing disposition is the one in the latest block that names it, whichever writer wrote that block. 05-intent says the standing is the latest reading-occasioned block where one exists, otherwise the latest verdict block, and that a verdict overrides a reading-occasioned block only where its rationale names that block's occasion, wherever the two sit in the section. A later verdict that does not name the occasion is the standing under the first rule and is overridden under the second, so the fold, and whatever reads a condition's standing from it, differs.
