2026-10-23 — Release hold, reading of Q1 (facilitator on delegation, iss-2610222014550692 round 3; reads dcn-2610230328220298): after the tag, or any tombstone, each withdrawal is re-armed by a copy of the person who recorded it (its By, the holder of the reservation commit that first recorded it, as the branch's history and the forge's record of pushes show), not by the reservation's current Abcd-Holder. Grounds: under the letter of Q1, another person's take-over followed by their --release would leave the first holder's withdrawals with nobody able to re-arm them, since a re-arm runs under the re-armer's own GitHub account and only the withdrawer's own copies re-arm. The tag sweep's every-act scope is unchanged.
