Every other stop while away is blocked, whatever stop_hook_active says, naming the next item, or with nothing owed asking the session to take the next ready item first and only then schedule its next look. The host's cap of eight consecutive continuations, or the cap its documented environment variable sets, is the outer bound, and the block abcd counts as reaching it records stopped with work owed in the away record. The old row that allowed an unchanged owed set once stop_hook_active was true reproduced the stall, since a reply of awaiting the person with no tool call ended the turn until morning.
