2026-11-26 — A person's own facilitator setting (itd-2611252049031692), their global facilitator.mode in ~/.abcd.noindex/config.json since the product thinker's ruling of 2026-11-26, is read only where HOME is the account's own home, the one the account database names for the account. A hosted environment whose home is its own is read as a computer of its own: what that home holds is that computer's setting, and the project's committed value stands above it. (As first recorded, this also held a person's per-project auto as human at the merge gate; the ruling removed the per-project entry, and dcn-2611262048349309 says what the gate does under the global auto.) Grounds: a session can run under a HOME the person did not set up. To reverse: remove the account-home comparison from the reading and its tests, in a change that records the reversal.
