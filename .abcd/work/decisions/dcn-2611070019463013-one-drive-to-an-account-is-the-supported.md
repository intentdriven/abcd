2026-11-07 — One drive to an account is the supported shape for a drive that may take a release. Drives on one account share the installed binary, so a move by one ends every other at its next comparison, whatever flags that other has, and on one profile a proof rewrites settings the other compares. A second drive is not refused: the ending is safe, since nothing unproved runs, the lane is let go and the next start runs the new binary, and drives in two checkouts with the updates turned off on both still work. A start that may take a release says so in a line. Revisit this if a person needs several drives moving on one account.
