2026-11-26 — The record-first rule for answering the person's questions about the project ships as its own bundled rules domain, ANSWERING, recalled on question phrases (where are we, is it possible, already built, who is working on, the plan forward), rather than as an eleventh rule in OPINIONS as iss-2611260623136583's remedy proposed. Grounds: OPINIONS recalls on words about approaches and conventions, so widening its recall to status questions would inject its whole set, and shrink it to its first rule under the budget, on every such prompt; a domain of its own injects exactly the one rule and can be silenced per repository with a dormant state. Reverse by moving the rule into OPINIONS and deleting the domain.
