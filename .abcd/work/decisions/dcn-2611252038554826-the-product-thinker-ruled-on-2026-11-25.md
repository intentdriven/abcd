2026-11-25 — The product thinker ruled on 2026-11-25 that a confirmed finding is always captured, whether they are present or away: asked whether a session that stops owing one is only flagged while they are present, they answered that the question should not need putting. So itd-2611251831138387 holds the stop in both cases, which reverses in part, for an owed finding alone, the rule of itd-2610170638184486 that a stop is refused only while they are away. That it is held once for each state of the list, so that a session cannot be caught in a loop, is the writing session's. To reverse: hold only while away.
