2026-11-28 — iss-2610142319015643: the stale-usage flag scan ends an invocation at a control operator read by shape, not by a shell parser. Semicolon, && and || count anywhere; a lone |, & or |& counts only as a word of its own; nothing inside quotes, brackets, braces, parentheses or an angle-bracketed placeholder counts, and a comment ends the scan. Grounds: command pages spell alternatives with the same characters (first|second, [--a | --b], "<id | text>"), and a comment glosses the invocation it follows (ideate.md: # private; --public only once ...); a comparison over every commands page and flag found no flag the old scan counted that the new one drops. A misread only withholds the stale-binary note, never grants one. Reverse by restoring the single last-occurrence check in synopsisInBlock and deleting controlOperators.
