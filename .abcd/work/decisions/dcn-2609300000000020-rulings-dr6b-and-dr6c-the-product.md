DR6b, verbatim: "(a) ONE AFTER ANOTHER BY DEFAULT: a step runs alongside earlier ones only if its plan says so; nothing already planned changes; reviews run side by side; the 2026-09-21 wording stands." A step without a `- needs:` line needs every earlier step, so itd-2609212103565953's wording of 2026-09-21 stands. DR6c, verbatim: "(c) FINISH, BUT HOLD THEM: when one piece is handed back, pieces in flight finish but nothing merges until the person re-plans; the person then decides whether the held pieces land as they are."
