2026-11-04 — A delta pair reviews a wording fix after a full pair, and nothing else. Every changed line is letters, digits and a sentence's punctuation, naming no record of any family and no file and holding no code; a corrected id or path takes a full pair. The list of where such a fix may fall is closed and in the build of abcd that runs land: markdown under docs/explanation/, docs/how-to/ and docs/tutorials/, the plans and roadmap of the design record, and resolved and wontfix issue records. Open issues and decisions stay off it, because later steps read those bodies, so a change to them takes a full pair, and so does a ledger record's grounds section, which is structured and append-only. No file is added, removed or moved, and every file keeps its frontmatter byte for byte. No part of a listed path starts with a dot or begins as the name of a host's instruction file does. Everything else takes a full pair.
