2026-10-16 — A fork origin refuses reservations (facilitator-on-delegation, drain-many-accounts): a fresh reservation and a drain's start ask the forge once per process whether a github.com origin is a fork, and refuse a fork, naming the repository it was made from and the command that sets origin to it; renewals and releases never ask, an origin that is not on github.com is not asked, and a forge that does not answer gives a warning and lets the act through, so a forge outage never stops leases being kept (grounds: a fork's reservation branches are invisible to every other account, so a hold there protects no one; iss-2610161015400509).
