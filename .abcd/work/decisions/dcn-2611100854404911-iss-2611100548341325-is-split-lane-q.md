2026-11-10 — iss-2611100548341325 is split. Lane q builds an audit of the git calls the hooks reach with no deadline, with a checked-in baseline counting today's reached calls. The audit refuses a reached call not in the baseline and a count above the baseline's, and fails on an entry reached fewer times or not at all until it is lowered. A line added to the baseline by hand is caught only by review of the baseline's diff; a gate refusing an added line against the merge base is a later step of the issue. Later lanes bind the named reads, then the rest, lowering and removing baseline entries as they go. The docs say 'every' only once the baseline is empty. Grounds: about 800 context-free call sites, of which the reached subset is unknown; a ratchet is the usual way to migrate a large codebase step by step without a flag day. Reverse: delete the baseline and make the audit strict.
