A refused question is handed back for the interviewer to redraft, and anything that does end an interview tells the person in plain words what happened and what to do. The ruling reverses the written interview's stop-on-refusal behaviour (the brief line 'the interview stops' in internal/core/interview/written.go), and the fix is tracked by the issue captured the same day for the terminal route.
