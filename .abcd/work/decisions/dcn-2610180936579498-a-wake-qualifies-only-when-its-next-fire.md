2026-10-18 — A wake qualifies only when its next fire time falls before the session's earliest renewal due, and a background task never counts (facilitator-on-delegation, itd-2610180912189125 review round 1; refines dcn-2610180913421109): a session_crons entry qualifies when the next time its cron expression matches after now, read in the machine's local time, is no later than the earliest renewal due among the live reservations under this copy's key minus the lease skew allowance, a renewal being due halfway between a reservation's claim or last renewal and its expiry; with no live reservation it must fire within half the default lease. The next fire time comes from a small five-field cron parser of abcd's own (minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week, with lists, ranges, steps and the wildcard), since the host documents schedule as a cron expression and a new dependency would need sign-off; an expression it cannot parse never qualifies. A background task does not count on its own while the person is away, since a shell task such as a long sleep or a tail that never ends reports as in flight however distant its wake. So a cron at 09:00 seen at 21:00, or a wake that fires after a lease lapses, no longer passes (grounds: the plan review of 2026-10-18, finding 3; code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks session_crons and background_tasks fields; internal/core/lease; AGENTS.md new dependencies need sign-off)
