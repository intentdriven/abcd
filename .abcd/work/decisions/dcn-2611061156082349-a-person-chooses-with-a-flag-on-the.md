2026-11-06 — A person chooses, with a flag on the command they type, how many marks a drive tolerates before it ends. A mark here is a drive's stop on a changed or unreadable run state, which is distinct from a person's stop; this leaves a person's stop as it is. The default stays one, as built: the first such stop ends the drive (`.abcd/development/brief/04-surfaces/35-drain.md:325`), and the flag relaxes that rule and nothing else. Below the limit a marked run is set aside, nothing goes on from what it says, and the drive goes on to other work. A later drive, step or hand-in still refuses that run until a person discards its lanes or restores its state. This rules on a draft intent not yet filed, and none of it is built.
