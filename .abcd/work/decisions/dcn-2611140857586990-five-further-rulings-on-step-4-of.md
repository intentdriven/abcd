2026-11-14 — Five further rulings on step 4 of spc-2610301925429250, made by the technical facilitator on delegation; they add to dcn-2611140657258823 and dcn-2611140407301478, and replace the comparison and the reading of an earlier run that dcn-2611140657258823 describes. First, the loop never saves a run its own read would refuse: every save of the run state runs the read's checks of the stored models on what it would write, and a change that would fail them is refused as that change, naming where, with the file already there left as it stands. Second, a model the loop learns after reviewers were handed out (a runner's report reaching a receipt a step collected before it, or the model the landing takes for its trailer) is a writer from that moment: the writers each reviewer records are recomputed in the same write, and a reviewer of the open round that any model it holds no longer differs from is withdrawn, its verdict not counted, and handed out again by the same move, or the lane is handed back as stuck where its round is settled or the lane has gone on. Third, every model a runner was started on for the lane's implementer, fix or conflict-resolution role is a writer whether or not a receipt came back, so a restarted lane keeps the gone agent's model among its writers where a runner started it; the writers are the union of those launched models, the runners' reports and the receipts' known names. Fourth, two names are other models only when each names exactly one family on a closed list kept in internal/core/matrix/samemodel.go and the families differ, or the family is the same and both carry a version whose digits are not a prefix of the other's; qualifier words, carriers, snapshots and date stamps, sizes, aliases with no family word and names holding two families are the same model, and a writer or class whose name holds no listed family hands the lane back. Fifth, a run read as started before reviewers' models were recorded keeps the existing mark and no version field, since a version field is as editable as the mark: what holds is that its pull request body, run record and text say in words that the reviewer's models were not recorded, and a reviewer it hands out after the read is compared and recorded as any other. Grounds: a doubtful comparison counts as the same model, since a change reviewed by its writer's model while the record names another is the costly failure; a record names what ran; and a file the loop writes must be one it reads. To reverse: replace the family list or the comparison in internal/core/matrix/samemodel.go, the writers' union or resettleWriters in internal/core/implement/loop/reviewers.go, or modelsSavable in internal/core/implement/loop/state.go, in a change that says why.
