2026-11-11 — Land's reader of dependency lines refuses the pull request body lines the next sentence names, prose included; the refusal names the line by number, gives the accepted shape and tells the author to reword the sentence. A line that is not an accepted line is refused when, with the characters the scan drops removed (format characters, non-spacing and enclosing marks, three Hangul fillers, NUL and backslash), it holds, in any case (the long s, U+017F, counts as s), depends-on with an ASCII hyphen anywhere, or depend or depends, then any run, or none, of hyphens, underscores, ASCII spaces, tabs, Unicode dashes (category Pd), minus signs (U+2212) or hyphen bullets (U+2043), then on, then any run, or none, of ASCII spaces or tabs and nothing else, then a colon. Any other line is read as declaring none, among them: a line with anything else between the words, such as another word or a no-break space (It depends heavily on: the queue), or between on and the colon, such as a number (This depends on #5: it needs the schema); a line with a spacing mark (category Mc), such as U+0903, inside a word; a line with no colon whose words are not depends-on with an ASCII hyphen, such as words joined by a space, a spaced hyphen, an underscore or another kind of hyphen or dash, U+2011 and the en dash among them (Depends on #5, Depends - On #5, depends_on #5); and a line with other wording for a dependency (Blocked by #5, Requires #5). A dependency is declared only by an accepted line, so write the accepted line. This corrects dcn-2611101637220432 on the refusal's wording, which that record gives as it stood before this one.
