2026-11-06 — The technical facilitator withdrew the rule that a second cancellation of a check for the same reason stops a pull request and opens a speed lane. This supersedes the cancel policy of dcn-2609280000000001 and its amendment dcn-2611062059314000, and the line of dcn-2610280635092222 that rules out a further raise; the warning those records set stays. A merge-queue entry dropped with no failing test is re-armed until it lands, and a real failure gets fixed. The check job's limit exists only to free a stuck queue and does not judge speed, so it sits just under the queue's own 90-minute check timeout, which does not change: the job's ceiling is 85 minutes, the job clock's watchdog fires at 80, and each package's test limit is 70, so a package that hangs early still fails with a goroutine dump at its own limit and one that starts late meets the watchdog first. A run nearing the ceiling is reported by the warning line, at 75, and answered by speed work. By the same reasoning a shard of the macOS suite, whose limit frees a scarce runner and runs on no queue's clock, takes a ceiling of 90, a watchdog at 85, a warning at 80 and a package limit of 80: its slowest package took about 37 minutes on its own shard, hosted macOS runners vary about twofold, and a release cut needs the suite green, so a slow runner may not fail a cut with no failing test. The local gate's budgets stay as they are, so these two jobs no longer share its 40-minute package limit.
