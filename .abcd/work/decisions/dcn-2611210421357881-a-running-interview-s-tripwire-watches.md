2026-11-21 — A running interview's tripwire watches the pre-push gate's judgement of this checkout's HEAD commit rather than the files that judgement rests on (iss-2611012144422634, iss-2610040739124513). Before and after each dispatch it asks whether .githooks/pre-push would let a push of HEAD through with no new preflight, by the gate's own rule: a receipt for HEAD in the local tier of any worktree git lists, a file once links are followed, in a tier git tracks nothing in, or a remote-tracking ref outside every receipt-less prefix holding HEAD. It stops the interview only when that goes from no to yes, naming the receipt or the ref in plain words. So a role that plants a receipt in a sibling's tier, registers a worktree holding one, or plants a remote-tracking ref at HEAD is stopped again, while a peer's fetch, commit or preflight on its own commit is not; a peer's preflight on the very commit this checkout is at does open the gate and stops the interview, since abcd cannot tell it from a role's write. The receipt-less prefixes are mirrored in Go from the hook's table, and a test runs the hook's own receiptless_tracking against the mirror. A sibling worktree entry that appears during a dispatch carrying its own config.worktree, a commondir leading to another common directory, or no commondir stops the interview too, which also catches an entry removed in one turn and made again in the next; git worktree add writes none of these unless the repository turns on per-worktree configuration, whose copied config.worktree then stops it. A loose ref that a peer's packing removes between its finding and its read is read once in the packed refs. dcn-2611210303400310 says a planted ref moves no default push; that is false for a remote-tracking ref, which the gate counts as already pushed, and this decision corrects it. The receipts in this checkout's own tier stay hashed as dcn-2611210343424922 says, since they gate a push of any of its commits, not HEAD's alone.
