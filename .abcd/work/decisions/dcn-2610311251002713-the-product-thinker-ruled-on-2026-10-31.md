2026-10-31 — The product thinker ruled on 2026-10-31, on iss-2610311224444576, that an issue's reservation is narrowed at the source: reserving an issue lane holds its found_at and the paths its fix changes, widened before the lane edits a further path and backing off a live hold another copy has there, and a reservation made by hand keeps the footprint it names. Found after the ruling: `abcd build <iss-N>` and the drain already reserve an issue's found_at alone, and the 36-path hold iss-2610311224444576 records carries no run stage, so it was made by hand; this ruling does not reach it, and how a hand reservation wider than its record is treated goes back to the product thinker. Rejected: flagging an over-wide hold while leaving it in force, since builds stay blocked; and lapsing a hold with no run stage sooner, since a paused holder would lose its cover.
