2026-10-31 — The product thinker ruled on 2026-10-31, on iss-2610311224444576, that an issue's reservation is narrowed at the source: reserving an issue lane holds its found_at and the paths its fix changes, widened as the lane changes them, and a reservation made by hand keeps the footprint it names. Rejected: flagging an over-wide hold while leaving it in force, since builds stay blocked; and lapsing a hold with no run stage sooner, since a paused holder would lose its cover.
