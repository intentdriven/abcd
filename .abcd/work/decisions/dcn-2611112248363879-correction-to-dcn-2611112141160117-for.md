2026-11-11 — Correction to dcn-2611112141160117 for iss-2611101318042398: the page reading, mdrecord.ReadPage, reads a fence indented four columns or more as text from its opener and reports it, under a list item or not. Wherever the top-level, list-nested and list-item readings mask a line differently, or the page may end a fence or comment elsewhere than the reading does (a line inside it nearer the margin than its list item's text, a comment opened after text and not closed in the same paragraph, a fence opener on a list marker's line, a fence opener in an HTML block, a comment opener in a pre, script, style or textarea element), that line and every line below it are read as text and one layout statement names the first. So every line is read or lies in a span all three readings agree on. The open-questions reader takes its section, items and settled markers from the same page text.
