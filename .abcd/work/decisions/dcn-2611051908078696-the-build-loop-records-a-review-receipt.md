2026-11-05 — The build loop records a review receipt only from a pair that read the pushed head, its records commit included. Where the mode is auto, a records commit takes a review round of its own, scoped to that commit, before anything is pushed; a round that does not pass parks the lane at its pull request for a person, with no fix round. By the coordinator's reading, this meets the reason behind clause 6 of dcn-2610291524030570 and the full-pair ruling in dcn-2611012113436014; the person is told and can overrule. It supersedes that clause's statement that the loop writes no receipt.
