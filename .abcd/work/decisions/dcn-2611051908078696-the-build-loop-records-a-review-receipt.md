2026-11-05 — The build loop records a review receipt only from a pair that read the pushed head, its records commit included. Where the mode is auto, a lane's full round is told each resolution it declares and judges it, and the records commit then takes a review round of its own, scoped to that commit, before anything is pushed, unless the commit moves an issue's record that round was not told of or holds more than plain markdown, when the lane parks before any round. A round that does not pass parks the lane at its pull request for a person, with no fix round, and a parked lane is not synced. By the coordinator's reading, this meets the reason behind clause 6 of dcn-2610291524030570 and the full-pair ruling in dcn-2611012113436014; the person is told and can overrule. It supersedes that clause's statement that the loop writes no receipt.
