2026-11-13 — Before step 2 of the spot-check spec sends any record, it settles whether a record's free text is sent at all or only its typed fields. The cleaning rule keeps a bare relative path made of letters, digits, dots, underscores, dashes and slashes, and it reads a word by its shape alone, so what it keeps is decided on the writing machine and cannot be narrowed after a record is written. Grounds: records are never edited, and the card step 3 draws takes the mode, the author's access, the approval and file names from typed fields and the issue record, not from stored reasons.
