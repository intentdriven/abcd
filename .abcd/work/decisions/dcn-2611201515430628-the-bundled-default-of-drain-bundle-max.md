2026-11-20 — The bundled default of drain.bundle_max is 1 until both the split of a failed bundle and the drain's figures have landed (steps 5 and 6 of spc-2611201455051897), and whichever of the two lands last raises it to 5, the default the person set (dcn-2611201429107457), for itd-2611201450052326. Grounds: before the split, a bundle that fails as a whole would hand every member to a person with its tombstone; before the figures, the first forty issues the measurement counts would pass uncounted. A person who sets a higher cap in a config file before then takes the whole-lane hand-back step 4 describes. Decided by the technical facilitator on delegation, 20 Nov 2026.

Amended 20 Nov 2026 (dcn-2611201605030535): its second ground no longer holds, since the forty-issue comparison is counted by hand from the forge and would not pass uncounted before the figures land. The first ground, that a bundle failing as a whole before the split would hand every member to a person, carries the decision alone.
