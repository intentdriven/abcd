2026-11-24 — The product thinker ruled on 2026-11-24 how work divides between the two roles. The product thinker owns the brief and the judgement of what good looks like, and is never shown technical matters: security triage, gate and pipeline design, queue and CI settings, worktree upkeep, code naming, test-proof standards and advisory texts all stay off that role's surfaces. Under facilitator.mode auto every technical decision is automated, by agents and peer sessions, and only the most critical decisions reach a person. Outside auto, the technical facilitator is shown what a technical generalist can judge, not a specialist's detail: a security or backend verdict stays with its specialist reviewer, and the facilitator approves a plain summary of it. Applied the same day: fixed advisories publish at the cut after an automated check under auto, and after one read of the batch by the facilitator otherwise; two lane-plan questions on away-mode behaviour and drain-bundle order were settled by the facilitator role rather than asked.
