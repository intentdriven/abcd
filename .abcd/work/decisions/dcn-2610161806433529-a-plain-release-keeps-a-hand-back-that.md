2026-10-16 — A plain release keeps a hand-back that still stands (facilitator-on-delegation, drain-many-accounts review round): a release with no hand-back of its own reads the reservation branch's first-parent line below its tip, at most 256 commits, to the first tombstone, and when that tombstone marks a hand-back whose record origin's default branch holds unchanged by the reading handBackStands uses, the new tombstone carries its kind and blob forward and the result says so (Result.HandBackKept); the walk passes renewals and take-overs, and an unreadable commit, a plain tombstone, the root or the bound keep nothing. The carried tombstone names the person releasing as its holder, since the closed trailer form names no earlier holder and a new trailer would read as unreadable to every binary already released. A release asked to hand back over a tip a plain tombstone already released publishes the hand-back as that tombstone's child, fenced by Tip on the tombstone or the reservation it released; with HandBackBlob it publishes none once the record's blob is another. Reserving and releasing an issue is not a person's change to its record, and a failed build start's clean-up is not a person's decision, so neither erases the hand-back (grounds: dcn-2610161040365515, dcn-2610161412283450; spc-2610161044461448 step 3; the review of steps 1 to 18).
