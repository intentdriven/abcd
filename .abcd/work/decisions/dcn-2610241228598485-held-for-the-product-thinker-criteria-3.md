2026-10-24 — Held for the product thinker: criteria 3 and 5 of itd-2610240823055889 conflict when the assistant's settings file exists. Criterion 3 makes the status line (the assistant's settings) its own person-approved question, and criterion 5 allows four questions (download, shell settings, plan, identity) plus one per tool, so a fresh account can be asked five. The build keeps the status-line question separate, the side on which nothing changes without a yes, and the spec close records criterion 5 as not met in that case until the product thinker rules: count the status line in the budget, fold it into the download question, or move it onto the plan screen.
