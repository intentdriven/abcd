2026-10-04 (the product thinker, working hypothesis, not a ruling): a brief is the product thinker's target design, written in present tense as the vision of the finished product; abcd makes it real step by step through the technical facilitator, deterministically: each brief section lists the intents and implementations that realise it, and a brief change is assessed for a new intent or issue, or for a contradiction with a filed, planned or built intent, which owes the product thinker a design decision. Under test on another repository; a state-of-the-art pass follows. Recorded on itd-61, itd-142 and itd-143.
