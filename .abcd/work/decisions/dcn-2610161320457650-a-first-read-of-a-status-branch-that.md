2026-10-16 — A first read of a status branch that runs past the walk limit is shown as doubtful only while something beneath what it read could still stand: when the oldest commit it read does not read as an announcement, or was sent, by the time it carries, less than a day and an hour before the read (the longest life plus C9's one-hour slack), refining dcn-2610161100416718 (spc-2610150728396006 step 3 fix round 2, decided by the facilitator on delegation). Status branches only grow, so every new checkout's first read of a long-lived one passed the limit and showed a doubt for history that had all ended. The bound is the oldest commit's send time rather than its own expiry, since a commit beneath it, sent earlier with a longer life, can outlast it.
