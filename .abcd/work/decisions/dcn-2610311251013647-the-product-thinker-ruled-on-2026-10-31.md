2026-10-31 — The product thinker ruled on 2026-10-31, on iss-2610311224587853, that the board's Next list shows only intents that pass the build's record-only pre-start checks; an intent that fails one (a spec with no footprint, an open question, a hold, an unsettled blocker, no step left) is listed under Later with the failing check named, as the pick head already treats it, and the issue is fixed as a defect rather than planned as an intent. Rejected: keeping such intents in Next marked with their failing check, since Next would mix buildable and blocked work; and promoting the issue to an intent, since Next would mislead until it shipped.
