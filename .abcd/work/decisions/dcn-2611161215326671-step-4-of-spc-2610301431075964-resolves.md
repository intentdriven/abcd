2026-11-16 — Step 4 of spc-2610301431075964 resolves an invocation's command path as the longest run of words naming a command, and reports the word the walk stops at as a sub-command the tree lacks only when the command reached has sub-commands, the word has a command name's shape (lower-case letters joined by single hyphens), and the command's usage line declares no positional argument that may be such a word; decided by the run for owner review

Grounds: A misspelt or removed verb is the likeliest stale claim, so the walk must check the word after the root, whose own argument is a record id. The facts hold no positional-argument types, so the usage line is read: A placeholder naming an id, a tag, or free text (`<record-id>`, `<itd-N|iss-N>`, `<release-tag>`, `[tag]`, `[text]`) takes no command-shaped word, since ids and tags carry digits, and capture and intent refuse a lone word, and a word before a record id, as a misspelt sub-verb. Any other placeholder, such as mode's `[<state>]`, does, so `abcd mode managed` passes. Of the committed commands that have sub-commands, only mode takes such a word. A placeholder after a flag is that flag's value, and the line is read up to its first alternative form.
