2026-11-27 — A lane a drive sets aside because a live driver holds its run, at the claim of its opening or of a take-over, keeps its reservation's passive renewals, and so does the kept hold of a run whose signal's stop could not be applied while another live driver holds it: the drive writes no left-to-lapse note for either. Grounds: step 3's acceptance leaves to lapse a hold no drive is working, and a live driver is working that run; a note there would let the lease lapse under a session whose long command has no step, so a peer could reserve the issue and that session's landing be refused. Only a hold no one works is left to lapse: a take-up refused, the landing's missing record, a kept hold whose driver is gone. Reverse by writing the note at every set-aside site again (setLaneAside and keepUnstopped in internal/core/implement/loop/terminal.go) and inverting TestARunWithALiveDriverInAnotherTerminalIsSetAside (itd-2611141610197753).
