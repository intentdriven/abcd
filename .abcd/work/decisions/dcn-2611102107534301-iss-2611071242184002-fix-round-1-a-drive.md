2026-11-10 — iss-2611071242184002 fix round 1: a drive counts every receipt it consumes as one refused step under one stage, check and reason (receipt; refused for the work), whatever the receipt lacked; the receipt's own gaps and the digest of the bytes judged stay in the run record's line only. Grounds: the drain's count of identical refusals restarted whenever the reason differed, and the reason carried the receipt's own gaps, so with no bound an implementer that failed a new way each time was never handed back; the drive's count of failed launches lives in memory and was lost on a restart. The count now lives in the lane's existing refused field, so the third consumed receipt in a row is stuck with or without a bound, a fresh drive on the same state carries it on, and the state's shape is unchanged. The fifteen-minute quiet restarts only the stuck minutes, never this count, so the time between consumed receipts does not matter. A step of the lane that succeeds clears the count, and a refusal of another kind starts its own, as for every refusal the drain counts. To reverse: count each consumed receipt under its own refusal again in consumeRefused (internal/core/implement/loop/receipt_standing.go).
