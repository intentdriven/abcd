2026-10-29 — The product thinker ruled on 29 October 2026 that an intent's Audit Notes section carrying no reviewer's marker counts as audited only when it reads as a real audit, one that names the acceptance criteria and gives a verdict on them; a ruling's amendment line or any other note that does not is not an audit, and the intent still owes its review. The retrospective seed and the list of reviews owed judge this by one shared rule (intent.HandAudited), so they cannot disagree about a record.
