2026-11-20 — A build now reserves only the steps in flight: its start reserves the spec step it opens next, the reservation widens to each later step's packages as that step's lane opens, and once a step lands the next renewal narrows it to what the steps still in flight need (their packages, the paths their lanes widened to, the files their lanes' branches changed, and the spec record each landing marks). A lane widens only to a path the hold does not already cover, so an edit to a file under a landed step's packages leaves no record but the lane's branch; and narrowing waits only while a lane has been handed an implementer or a fix round and has not reached its landing with nothing pending, since its branch shows its edits only once its receipt commits them and two automated lanes must never edit one file. A lane still at its worktree or brief has edited nothing and holds nothing back, and a resume never takes back a path the narrowing gave up: while a lane is mid-edit it keeps only what the reservation still names. This is the second rule dcn-2611012049050703 records, widening on opening and narrowing on landing, built as the only behaviour in place of that decision's default of narrowing to the steps not yet landed; no per-repository setting is read, and offering the other rule as one stays open in iss-2610230839002949. The narrowing on landing is what keeps two runs whose step orders cross from each holding a package the other's next step needs while waiting on the other; a lane whose opening waits on another holder's hold waits alone, and the run's other lanes go on.
