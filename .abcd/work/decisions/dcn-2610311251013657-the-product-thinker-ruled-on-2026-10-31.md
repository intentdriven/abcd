2026-10-31 — The product thinker ruled on 2026-10-31, on iss-2610311225001256, that a peer copy holding a record in an earlier lifecycle bucket than this checkout, with edits of its own, is reconciled rather than waited out or ignored: the refusal names the copy, its branch, its last commit's age, and the reconcile move (rebase that copy onto the default branch, which carries its edits onto the record at its current path, after which it holds the same bucket and no longer blocks), and abcd never rewrites another copy itself; a copy that cannot be reconciled cleanly keeps blocking and goes to the person. The product thinker asked whether such a copy could be merged into the plan; this rule is that answer, and it is open to revision. Rejected: an advisory refusal alone, since the build stays blocked; ageing a copy out of the check, since its edits are silently overtaken; and never counting an earlier bucket, since fresh draft edits are overtaken too.
