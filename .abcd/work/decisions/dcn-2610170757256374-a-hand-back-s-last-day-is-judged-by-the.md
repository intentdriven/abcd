2026-10-17 — A hand-back's last day is judged by the tombstone's Abcd-Released-At (facilitator-on-delegation, drain-many-accounts, step 18): abcd peers lists a hand-back released within 24 hours of the reader's clock, and never one dated more than lease.Skew ahead of it, which is left out rather than counted from when it was fetched (grounds: the release stamps Abcd-Released-At on every tombstone it writes, so it is the time the hand-back was made; a date ahead is not trusted, and leaving it out means a fast clock cannot keep it on show past its day, while the drain's own reading of the hand-back is unchanged; refines dcn-2610161040394970).
