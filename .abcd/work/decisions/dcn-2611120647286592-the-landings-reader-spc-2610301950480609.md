2026-11-12 — The landings reader (spc-2610301950480609, itd-2610301850454860) counts a recorded approval of a pull-request row's merged head only where the forge's answer for that pull request was read and says the pull request merged as the row's commit; with the answer unread the row is unattended whatever the local tier holds, and a later listing that reads the forge settles it. A recorded approval of a push row's own commit counts as before. A merge under the human mode whose answer was not read carries the rule "forge unread", or the drain rule where its branch carries the drain's admission and it is classed drain. The causes of an unread row are examples, among them the listing's time running out, a refusal that stops the asking, the listing's bound on asking again spent, a failed call, an error on the pull request's node, and an answer too large to read. This replaces the clause of dcn-2611120632175235 that a recorded approval of the merged head still counts and its sentence that such a merge carries the rule "forge unread", and reads its list of causes as examples. Grounds: one rule for every row whose forge answer was not read, so each such row is unattended alike and a later listing settles each alike. To reverse it, read the recorded approval of the merged head again whether or not the forge's answer was read, in classify (internal/core/landings/landings.go).
