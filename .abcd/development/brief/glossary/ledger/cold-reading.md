# cold-reading

A **cold reading** is what the four positions (widening, entailment, comparative, detection) perform. Cold names two things at once: command of established patterns, which is what makes an item assessable, and absence of investment in the framing, which is what makes the contribution possible. A reading with only the second is naivety, not expertise.

## When to use

Use it for the instrument's act: the assembler builds the visible world, the definition supplies the object and the question, the reading returns items, and the ingest verb validates them.

## When NOT to use

Do not call a cold reading a review or an audit: a review weighs and recommends, and a reading is forbidden both. The fidelity audit of a shipped intent is warm work and keeps its own name.
