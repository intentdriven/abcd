# position

A **position** is where a reading stands in the loop, and it fixes what the reading may produce. The four are the widening reading (candidate configurations the construal admits), the entailment reading (claims the frame already carries), the comparative reading (candidates characterised against declared criteria) and the detection pass (gaps between the shipped tree and the claim record). The position resolves to a definition and to a regime; an operator selects a position and never a regime.

## When to use

Use it for the operand of the assembler and the field on a reading record. Four positions, four definitions, one blindness core.

## When NOT to use

Do not call a position a phase or a step of the record's roadmap; those are delivery vocabulary.
