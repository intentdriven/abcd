# lapse

A **lapse** is evidence, not confession. The causal claim behind the ledger is that recording at the point of commitment prevents retrospective reconstruction, and the lapse log is the evidence that bears on that claim. The three opening entries are written at the outset rather than discovered: the pre-tooling window, anticipation, and commitments made outside the tooling.

## When to use

Use it for a discipline that gave way, with the point in the process at which it did.

## When NOT to use

Do not capture a code defect as a lapse; a lapse is about the record's discipline, not about the tree.
