# Product — The Why and What

## Identity (canonical)

The single recorded home for how abcd names itself. Every public surface —
README strapline, plugin manifest and catalogue descriptions, the AGENTS.md
opening, the CLI banner and the site's hero — renders from these fields; a
surface that says something else is drift (iss-143's finding), fixed by
re-rendering from here or by deliberately changing this block. The lockup is
the first three lines a reader meets: the name with its descriptor, the
tagline, and the purpose. The title spells out the name, the pitch says
what abcd is technically, and the listing is the line a tool listing shows
beside other tools, written whole.

- **Name:** abcd
- **Title:** Agent-Based Configuration for Development
- **Descriptor:** the open way to build your idea, one working prototype at a
  time
- **Tagline:** Your idea evolves. The record keeps up.
- **Purpose:** For people who know what they want to build, and want every
  prototype to match it.
- **Pitch:** Keeps your idea, its decisions, and every check as plain files
  beside the code.
- **Listing:** The open way to build your idea with the agent you already use.
  abcd keeps your idea, its decisions, and every check as plain files beside
  the code.

This chapter states what abcd IS for the people who use it: the press release that
frames the product, the context it grew from, the mental model everything else
rests on, the scope it bounds, and the personas that voice intent quotes. Read
`03-mental-model.md` first — the brief / intent / spec distinction, with the
spec's steps, the bundle and the derived release beside it, underpins every other
section of the brief.
