# Personas

Customer quotes use placeholder personas from `.abcd/development/personas.json` — Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave, Eve, Frank, Grace, Henry, Iris, Jack, Kira, Liam, Maya, Nia — each with role hints (solo founder, staff engineer, product manager, etc.); the registry is canonical, and the roster continues that sequence as it grows. Selection is by role, never by name: an intent's audience picks the role, and the role determines the registered name. A quote may instead be attributed to a role alone, with no name ("said a product thinker who had planned forty-eight intents"): a nameless role invents no name, so it needs no registry entry, and any name that is used must be registered and match the role ([itd-79](../../intents/disciplines/itd-79-persona-registry.md), ruled in [dcn-2610271112330536](../../../work/decisions/dcn-2610271112330536-the-product-thinker-accepted-on.md)).

**Codified abcd principle:** never use real names in press releases (PII), never use generic "a hypothetical user" phrasing (loses voice). A named placeholder or a specific role keeps a quote grounded without leaking identifiers: a quote attributed to one of abcd's own roles, such as "said a technical facilitator who ran one intent through a coordinator", is not generic phrasing, because the role gives the quote its voice ([dcn-2610271112330536](../../../work/decisions/dcn-2610271112330536-the-product-thinker-accepted-on.md)).

See [`04-surfaces/05-intent.md` § 4](../04-surfaces/05-intent.md#4-persona-registry) for the surface-level integration: `/abcd:intent "<text>"` picks a persona for the customer quote in each press release.
