# The selection criteria are a declared, recorded discipline — a candidate is characterised and selected against criteria the record states, never criteria supplied at invocation

## The rule

Selection at the gate weighs candidates against the criteria this record
declares, and only these. The comparative reading characterises each
candidate against each declared criterion; combining characterisations
into a choice is the maintainer's act, on grounds recorded at conjecture
granularity.

**Declared criteria** *(committed 2026-08-28, maintainer; amendable only
by ordinary discipline amendment, which is itself a recorded change)*:

- Plausibility — the conjecture could work by a mechanism we can state.
- Generativity — pursuing it opens further conjectures and questions
  rather than closing them.
- Cost — what building and carrying it consumes, stated before selection.
- Risk — what failing looks like, and what it would take down with it.
- Learning value — what is learned even if the conjecture fails.
- Practical importance — whether anyone's real work changes if it
  succeeds.

## The gate

- **Given** a comparative reading invocation, **when** its object is
  assembled, **then** the criteria come from this record and no criterion
  arrives at invocation.
- **Given** a selection recorded at the gate, **when** its grounds are
  read, **then** they speak to the declared criteria (or record why a
  criterion was set aside — a visible re-decision, never a silent one).

## Fit

The discipline family is the nearest existing home rather than an exact
fit — these criteria govern selection rather than delivery — and this
record says so deliberately, in preference to minting a record family for
one record.

## Staging

Lands with the record-side filings; consumed by the comparative reading's
definition. Amending the slate is an ordinary discipline amendment — a
recorded change, challengeable by any subsequent reading.
