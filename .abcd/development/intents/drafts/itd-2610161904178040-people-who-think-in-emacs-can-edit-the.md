# People who think in Emacs can edit the brief with Emacs keys

## Press Release

> People who think in Emacs can edit the brief with Emacs keys. On abcdesign.app, anyone in a live brief session can switch the brief editor to Emacs keys in the page's settings; it is off unless they turn it on, and it is remembered for them on that browser. With it on, the editor works the way an Emacs user's hands expect on every computer, Mac, Windows or Linux: Ctrl-Space sets the mark, Ctrl-W and Alt-W cut and copy, Ctrl-Y pastes, Ctrl-S searches, Ctrl-A and Ctrl-E go to the start and end of a line, Ctrl-K cuts to the end of the line. Alice, a solo founder who writes everything in Emacs, turns it on once and edits the press release without reaching for the mouse; Bob, who has never used Emacs, never sees it unless they turn it on.
