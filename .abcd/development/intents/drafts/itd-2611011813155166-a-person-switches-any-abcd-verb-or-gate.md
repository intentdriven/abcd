## Press Release

## Decisions

Answered by the product thinker on 2026-11-01.

1. **Who may switch what off is decided by whom a gate protects.** A gate that protects the project's own work (the secret scan before landing, the docs-fidelity check, the record gates) may be switched off by the repository's owner, for everyone working in it, and abcd shows that it is off on the board, in help and in the verb's own output. A protection that guards the person or their machine (the shell guard's hazards, such as refusing to remove the home directory, and the sandbox) has no off switch in this intent: a repository can never switch one off.
2. **The person needs no standing switch for their own protections.** The existing one-time allow (`abcd guard allow --once`, which only the person can run) covers a false refusal, and running unconfined is already a choice only the person's own home configuration can make (adr-2610161621415708); this intent adds neither.
