## Why This Matters

Every livery mark has a surface and earns it: the duckling, the lifeboat, and the two-by-two signal-flag icon are placed deliberately across abcd's surfaces instead of sitting as generated-but-unused assets. The lifeboat mark renders when disembark finishes packing a lifeboat and mirrors when embark unpacks one, so the direction of travel reads at a glance; the duckling appears as the mascot wherever the harness greets a human; the icon becomes the website and app icon. Settles the forge/web logo question itd-112 deferred as 'the existing logo stays for now'. Follows itd-133, which shipped the marks deliberately unwired, and consumes itd-112's colour ladder and emission discipline rather than minting its own.
