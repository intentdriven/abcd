# abcd records how each preflight on a machine behaved

## Press Release

> abcd keeps a record of how each preflight on a machine behaved: how long it waited for its turn and how long it ran, the CPU its whole run used, whether a test timed out, and how busy the machine was with other people's work at the time. The record is the person's own, kept on their machine under abcd's home, bounded in size and the same shape on macOS and Linux, and they can read it through one command. It is the evidence any later tuning of the machine's settings rests on, and the first case of the wider record of what abcd costs to run.
