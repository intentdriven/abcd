# Iteration 2 divergence register

The cold-reading instrument runs Iteration 2 exactly as the design framework
v4 and its readings companion v4 specify. Where the two documents disagree
the framework governs; the companion's ratified positions R1 to R7 are the
authors' later rulings and the framework's pending amendments. This note
lists every point at which the Iteration 2 materials (the planned intents,
their specs, the ADRs adopted on 2026-09-02, brief invariants 14 to 16 and
the decision-log entries of the same day) chose something the documents
leave open, with the section that is open, the choice, and the ground. A
point not listed here is one the documents settle and the materials follow.
Nothing here contradicts either document; a contradiction is a defect, not
a register entry.
