# Widen options, never recommend

**The rule.** At a conjectural question — one whose answer is a judgement the
human owns, not a fact the record can settle — an abcd surface **widens the
space and stays out of it**: it presents candidate construals (at most three,
a ceiling not a target), always includes the null answer ("none of these" /
"can't articulate yet"), and recommends nothing. Ordering, phrasing, and
defaults must not smuggle a preference; the tool's contribution is that the
human sees more of the space than they would have generated alone.

**Why.** An elicitation tool that recommends converts a question into a
confirmation: anchoring does the deciding and the human does the assenting,
and the record then carries the tool's frame with a human signature on it.
The value of machine participation at conjectural questions is coverage, not
selection — the same division of labour the record already runs elsewhere:
the itd-84 validator proposes routing and the human adopts it
(`verifier-selects-gates-decide`), and the interview's closing summary
pre-formats selection grounds without selecting. Widening is what a machine
can do without owning the judgement; recommending is owning it.

**Bounds.**

- Applies at *conjectural* questions. Where the record settles an answer
  (a fact, an existing decision, a lint result), stating it plainly is not a
  recommendation — it is reporting, and hedging it would be false balance.
- The ceiling is three construals because widening degrades into burying: a
  long menu is a recommendation by exhaustion. Fewer is always acceptable.
- The null answer is load-bearing, never decorative: a hold (per the itd-142
  hold register) is a first-class outcome, not a failure of the round.
- Presentation neutrality is part of the rule: no starred defaults, no
  "(recommended)" labels, no ordering by the tool's own scoring at
  conjectural questions.

**Promotion.** Underway (staged rung). The cold-reading output contract
carries a supply-regime field, and ingest checks each reading's output
against what its regime licensed it to produce: an evaluative output
containing an ordering, a score, or a single named recommendation is
refused; an explicative output in which a surfaced claim arrives already
dispositioned is refused; a registrative output attaching a proposed
resolution to a detection is refused. The check ships enforced per the ruling, with a
reserved degradation path: where a signature proves noisy in practice it
records the regime and flags for researcher review instead, and the
claimed property weakens from enforced to observed, said out loud per
`loud-staging`. (Enforced-at-birth is in tension with this principle's own
promotion rule — calibrated before it gates; the tension is recorded as
standing, and the ruled design governs the instrument meanwhile.)
